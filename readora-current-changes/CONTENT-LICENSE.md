# Sample content

The active catalog uses Aesop excerpts in `server/sourced-content.js` and verbatim excerpts from Lewis Carroll’s *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland* (1865; author died 1898) and Arthur Conan Doyle’s *The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes* (1892; author died 1930) in `server/varied-content.js`. The original literary texts are public domain in the USA and exceed the Philippine life-plus-50-year term. Each activity identifies its Project Gutenberg source and license. Assessment questions and feedback are AI-assisted project demo material requiring educator review.

The seven activities in `server/content.js` were written originally for this Readora project. You may use, modify, and redistribute these sample passages and questions with your project. They are not copied from a named literary source.

Before adding third-party texts, verify the exact work's license or public-domain status and record its author, source URL, and reuse basis in the exercise's source field. A site hosting a work does not by itself prove that every work on it is reusable.
